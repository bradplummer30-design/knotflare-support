For help with the game or with a purchase, e-mail plummerbro@gmail.com.

In the game: Settings, then PRIVACY & DATA, then EMAIL SUPPORT.

Your support e-mails

Support e-mails are used only to respond to and resolve your support request. They are kept for 90 days, and deleted earlier if you ask: send deletion requests to plummerbro@gmail.com.

How to play

You climb automatically. Press LEFT or RIGHT to jump to the next rope and stay ahead of the burning fuse. Catch a rope below its top; the short red tail at the bottom of a rope cannot be grabbed. Tapping the scenery does nothing. A Rotor Rig carries you upward for six seconds; the same two buttons steer it.

No sound

Check SOUND EFFECTS and SFX VOLUME, and MUSIC and MUSIC VOLUME, in Settings. Then check the device's volume, its silent mode and the audio output in use.

Paid outfits

Open OUTFIT SHOP. If prices are missing, check your connection; they appear by themselves while the Shop is open once the App Store can be reached. To get back outfits you bought before, press RESTORE PURCHASES at the bottom of the Outfit Shop while signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account that bought them. Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple.

Coins

Coins are collected on the ropes and unlock five outfits. You can earn 2,700 coins in total, exactly enough for all five. After that, coins no longer appear in runs. Coins you have saved stay spendable. Coins cannot be bought.

Progress

Progress is saved on your device. Updating the game keeps it. Deleting the game removes it, and the game cannot bring it back. RESTORE PURCHASES brings back paid outfits only.

Leaderboard

The HIGHEST CLIMB leaderboard uses Apple's Game Center and is optional. If it does not open, check that you are signed in to Game Center in the Settings app. The game plays normally without it.
