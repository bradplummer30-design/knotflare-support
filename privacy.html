KNOTFLARE Privacy Policy

Revision 3. Published and effective 17 November 2026.

KNOTFLARE is a climbing game for iPhone and iPad made by Bradly Plummer ("the developer").

Questions about this policy or your data: plummerbro@gmail.com

In short

  • You can play without any account and without a connection.
  • The game has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • There is no KNOTFLARE account and no KNOTFLARE server. The game does not send your progress or anything else to the developer.
  • Your progress is saved in a file on your device.
  • Two Apple services are used, and both are optional: Game Center, for the leaderboard, and the App Store, for paid outfits. Apple handles what they receive under Apple's own privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

What is saved on your device

The game saves your highest climb, run statistics, coin balance and lifetime earned coins, unlocked outfits, settings, tutorial status and daily-goal progress in a file on your device. If you use the leaderboard it also keeps, in the same file, the Game Center player identifier Apple gives this game, next to your best height and any score waiting to be sent.

This file is used only to run the game. The game does not send it to the developer.

  • Updating the game keeps the file.
  • Deleting the game removes the file, and the game cannot bring it back.
  • A backup of your device made by Apple (iCloud or a computer) may include the file. That backup is made and kept by Apple and by you, not by the game.

Game Center (optional)

The leaderboard is optional and gives no reward in the game.

Nothing is sent to Game Center until you press LEADERBOARD in the game for the first time. From then on:

  • the game signs in to Game Center by itself each time it starts, without asking again;
  • while you are signed in, it sends Apple your saved best height in whole metres (including a best you set before you first pressed LEADERBOARD) and the height of each run;
  • if a score cannot be sent, it waits on your device and is sent later, to the same Game Center account only.

To stop this, sign out of Game Center in the Settings app. You can keep playing; your local best, daily goal, coins and outfits do not depend on it.

Apple shows your best height with your Game Center name on its HIGHEST CLIMB leaderboard, to the audience your Game Center settings allow.

From Game Center the game receives whether you are signed in, the player identifier Apple gives this game, and confirmation that a score was accepted. It does not read your friends, your profile or the leaderboard table; Apple's own screen shows them. The developer runs no server that receives any of this. Like any player, the developer can see the names and heights on Apple's leaderboard.

Purchases (optional)

Six outfits can be bought as one-time purchases. Apple processes every purchase through the App Store. The game is not given your card or other payment details.

From Apple the game receives the outfits' names and prices and, for purchases made with your Apple Account, Apple's transaction records: which outfit, when, and Apple's proof that the purchase is genuine. The game uses them on your device to unlock your outfits and keeps a list of the unlocked outfits in its save file. The developer does not receive them from the game.

RESTORE PURCHASES (in the Outfit Shop) asks Apple again for your outfit purchases. It does not restore coins, coin outfits, records or daily-goal progress, which exist only in the file on your device.

Separately from the game, Apple gives every developer sales and financial reports for their apps (for example how many of each item were sold in each country). Apple decides what those reports contain.

Contacting support

Support is by e-mail. When you press EMAIL SUPPORT in the game, your own mail app opens with a message addressed to plummerbro@gmail.com. Nothing is sent unless you send that message yourself, and the game adds nothing to it.

If you send it, the developer receives what any e-mail carries: your e-mail address, your name if your mail app includes it, and whatever you write. The mailbox is a Gmail account, a service provided by Google.

  • Support e-mails are used only to respond to and resolve your support request.
  • Support e-mails are kept for 90 days.
  • They are deleted earlier if you ask. Send deletion requests to plummerbro@gmail.com.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

The game has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools, and it does not use the advertising identifier. It asks for no camera, microphone, contacts or location access.

What the game is built with

  • Unity (game engine). Unity's analytics, advertising and crash-reporting services are not part of the game.
  • Nice Vibrations by More Mountains, with the Lofelt haptics library, used to vibrate the device.
  • DOTween by Demigiant, used to animate the picture.
  • Sound effects made from Universal Sound FX by Imphenzia; original music.
  • Apple's Game Center and App Store frameworks, described above.

This website

This page and the support page are hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. They use no cookies, scripts or trackers of their own. GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement (https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The game opens these pages only when you press PRIVACY POLICY or SUPPORT in its Settings.

Children

The game does not ask for personal information and has no chat, messaging or user-made content. Game Center and App Store purchases follow the rules of the Apple Account in use, including any family or parental controls.
